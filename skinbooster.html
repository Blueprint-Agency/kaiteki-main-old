Skinbooster Treatment

For Hydration, Skin Repair & Natural Glow

Struggling with dull skin, fine lines, enlarged pores, or tired-looking complexion? Skinbooster treatments deliver deep hydration and skin repair from within — improving texture, elasticity, and radiance without changing your facial features.

Using advanced injectable formulations, Skinboosters work beneath the skin surface to restore moisture, stimulate collagen, and strengthen skin quality over time.

Tried Skincare, But Skin Still Looks Tired or Dehydrated?

You’re not alone. Many skin concerns can’t be fixed by creams alone:

Fine lines caused by dryness and collagen loss

Enlarged pores and uneven texture

Acne-damaged or sensitised skin

Skin that looks tired despite regular facials

Topical skincare works on the surface. Skinboosters work from within — improving skin quality at a cellular level.

How Skinbooster treatments work?

Skinboosters are injectable skin-repair formulations designed to hydrate deeply, stimulate collagen, and improve overall skin health — not volume or shape.

Each Skinbooster targets skin concerns in a slightly different way, allowing doctors to personalise treatment based on your skin condition.

Treatment Available

Plinest

  • Polynucleotide-based skin regeneration
  • Repairs damaged skin and improves elasticity
  • Ideal for stressed, ageing, or post-treatment skin

Profhilo

  • High-concentration hyaluronic acid
  • Deep hydration and skin remodelling
  • Improves firmness and overall glow

Rejuran

  • Salmon DNA (PN) technology
  • Repairs skin barrier and boosts healing
  • Suitable for sensitive, acne-prone, or damaged skin

Juvelook

  • Hybrid collagen-stimulating skinbooster
  • Improves pores, texture, and fine lines
  • Long-term skin quality enhancement

FAQ

Are Skinboosters suitable for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

Yes. Certain Skinboosters like Rejuran and Plinest are especially suitable for sensitive or damaged skin. Your doctor will assess suitability.

Is there any downtime?

Minimal. Mild redness or tiny injection marks may occur and usually subside within 24–48 hours.

How many sessions do I need?

Most patients require 2–3 sessions, followed by maintenance every 6–12 months, depending on skin condition and goals.
