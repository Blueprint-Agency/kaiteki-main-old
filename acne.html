What is Acne and Scars?

Acne scars are among the most common concerns patients seek advice for at our clinics in Malaysia. Scars, particularly those on the face, can deeply affect one's self-esteem and confidence. But fret not, at Kaiteki, our team is dedicated to helping you to get rid of acne scars.

There's a range of scar types and severities, making each treatment plan unique. The good news is that numerous methods can effectively treat acne scarring. However, the outcome largely depends on the specific type of scarring each patient is dealing with.

Type of Acne

Comedonal Acne

This type involves non-inflammatory lesions like blackheads and whiteheads.

Inflammatory Acne

It includes papules, pustules, and nodules, which are inflamed and may cause redness and swelling.

Cystic Acne

Deep, painful cysts form under the skin, often leading to severe inflammation and scarring.

Hormonal Acne

Typically seen in women, this type is triggered by hormonal changes, often leading to breakouts around the chin and jawline.

Type of Acne Scars

Ice Pick Scars

Deep, narrow scars that resemble small punctures.

Rolling Scars

Broad depressions with a wave-like appearance.

Boxcar Scars

Shallow to moderately deep depressions with well-defined edges.

Hypertrophic and Keloid Scars

Raised scars resulting from excess collagen production, often seen in severe acne cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best acne scar removal in Malaysia?

Lasers are considered the most effective for improving the appearance of acne scars, particularly boxcar and rolling scars. Ablative, fractional lasers usually work best, depending on your scar's size, thickness, and age.

Can you 100% remove acne scar?

Even with treatment, acne scars are difficult to heal. There's no 100% guarantee that the acne scars will be completely gone. But most treatments reduce the size of the acne scars and make them less visible.

What is the hardest acne scar to remove?

Keloids are generally caused by skin tissue growing over inflammation and are considered permanent. They are also the most challenging type of acne scar to treat.

Can acne scars be removed naturally?

Home remedies that people have traditionally used to treat acne scars include: black seed oil, which may help even out skin pigmentation. rosehip oil, which has helped reduce discoloration in postsurgical scars. honey, which may help wound healing and reduce future scarring.
