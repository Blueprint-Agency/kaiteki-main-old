How does Ultherapy work?

Unlike surface treatments, Ultherapy targets the SMAS layer — the same deep tissue addressed in a surgical facelift. Here's what happens during your session at Kaiteki:

Step 1: Skin Assessment & Ultrasound Imaging

Your doctor maps your skin layers in real time, pinpointing the exact depth for energy delivery.

Step 2: Energy Delivery to the SMAS Layer

Focused ultrasound targets the same deep tissue layer addressed in surgical facelifts — from the outside in.

Step 3: Collagen Remodelling Begins

Your skin starts rebuilding from within. New collagen forms gradually over the following weeks and months.

Step 4: Natural Lift Revealed

A firmer jawline, lifted brows, and tighter neck emerge progressively — looking refreshed, never overdone.

The Result?

No needles. No downtime. Just firmer, lifted skin that looks naturally younger — not altered.

FAQ

Is Ultherapy safe for Asian skin?

Yes. Ultherapy is FDA-cleared and safe for all skin types, including Asian skin. Our doctors customise energy settings based on your skin depth and tone to ensure precise, effective, and safe delivery — minimising any risk of pigmentation.

Does it hurt?

Most patients describe a tingling or warm sensation as the energy is delivered. Discomfort levels vary depending on the area treated. Our doctors can adjust settings and provide numbing options if needed to keep you comfortable throughout.

How many sessions will I need?

For most patients, one session per year is sufficient to maintain a visible lift. Depending on your skin condition and goals, your doctor may recommend a follow-up session or a combination with other treatments for enhanced results.

How is Ultherapy different from HIFU?

Both use focused ultrasound, but Ultherapy uses real-time ultrasound imaging to precisely visualise and target the correct tissue depth — making it more accurate and consistent. Ultherapy is also FDA-cleared, while many HIFU devices are not. Results from Ultherapy typically last longer (12–18 months vs 6–12 months for generic HIFU).

Can I combine Ultherapy with other treatments?

Absolutely. Ultherapy pairs well with dermal fillers, skin boosters (like Profhilo or Rejuran), Pico Laser, and radiofrequency treatments for a comprehensive rejuvenation plan. Your Kaiteki doctor will advise on the best combination for your skin.

Is there any downtime?

No downtime at all. You may experience mild redness or slight swelling for a few hours post-treatment, but you can resume your normal routine — including work and exercise — immediately. Sunscreen is advised for the first few days.
