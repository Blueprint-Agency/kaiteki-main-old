What is CoolSculpting?

Coolsculpting is also known as fat freezing whereby it uses a patented technology to freeze the fat cells non-invasively and eliminate it naturally for a better , contoured body.

In this modern era, everybody is trying to get into a healthier lifestyle. We have pilates, yoga , dance classes etc as people are more conscious about health and their body shapes. Hence, there is an increasing demand for non-invasive body contouring treatments.

Coolsculpting has been invented since 2008 when scientists discovered that sucking on popsicles can cause dimples due to the process of cryolipolysis. Later that year, Zeltiq started their clinical trials for the technology and was awarded FDA clearance for the technology. It has then taken the world by storm and it is one of the most performed body treatments in the world.

Popular CoolSculpting Treated Areas

Thighs

This is one the most common CoolSculpting areas, especially for women. Patients can eliminate fat from the inner thighs, outer thighs, or both!

Abdomen

Subcutaneous fat in the abdomen is quite hard to get rid of even if you have a healthy lifestyle but Coolsculpting can improve your body shape. Patients usually report high satisfaction rates for abdomen coolsculpting. Usually, at least 2 – 4 cycles will be needed.

Love Handles

Love is usually the opposite of what patients feel toward the fat that bears this romantic name. For people who want a more defined waist, this is something you might want to consider !

Bra fat and Back fats

No amount of exercise can eliminate these stubborn bulges but coolsculpting can !

CoolSculpting Treatment Experience

1. Design Phrase

The correct and suitable heights are determined, and markings are applied to the skin

2. Anesthesia

Local anesthesia is given to control pain

3. Start Process

3 small incisions are made on the upper eyelid. Excess fats can be removed if necessary.

4. Suturing

Stitching is performed, embedding non-absorbable sutures into the incisions.

5. Results

Permanent and noticeable results can be observed after 2 weeks of downtime.

What is the best acne scar removal in Malaysia?

Lasers are considered the most effective for improving the appearance of acne scars, particularly boxcar and rolling scars. Ablative, fractional lasers usually work best, depending on your scar's size, thickness, and age.

Can you 100% remove acne scar?

Even with treatment, acne scars are difficult to heal. There's no 100% guarantee that the acne scars will be completely gone. But most treatments reduce the size of the acne scars and make them less visible.

What is the hardest acne scar to remove?

Keloids are generally caused by skin tissue growing over inflammation and are considered permanent. They are also the most challenging type of acne scar to treat.

Can acne scars be removed naturally?

Home remedies that people have traditionally used to treat acne scars include: black seed oil, which may help even out skin pigmentation. rosehip oil, which has helped reduce discoloration in postsurgical scars. honey, which may help wound healing and reduce future scarring.
