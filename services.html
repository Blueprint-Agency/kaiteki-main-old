What can Derma V treat?

  • Pigmentation
  • Spider Veins
  • Rosacea
  • Skin Brightening

Picosure

The Picosure laser is a cutting-edge technology designed for various skin concerns. This laser uses ultra-short, high-intensity pulses of light energy to treat skin conditions effectively and efficiently.

When the Picosure laser is applied to the skin, the light energy is absorbed by the target, such as tattoo ink or pigmented lesions. This causes the target to break down into smaller particles that can be naturally removed from the body. At the same time, the high-intensity pulses also stimulate the production of collagen.

What can PicoSure treat?

  • Tattoo removal
  • Pigmented lesions
  • Acne scars
  • Wrinkles and fine lines
  • Birth marks
  • Pores

The PQX is known for its precision, safety, and ability to treat a wide range of skin types with minimal downtime. Co2 laser

CO2 Fractional Laser is a type of laser technology that is used to target specific areas of skin while leaving the surrounding healthy tissue untouched.

During the procedure, intense light energy is passed through micropulses and beams which create deep pinpointed penetrations in your skin, resulting in shorter recovery times than traditional laser treatments.

The laser encourages cellular growth and spread which supports new collagen and elastin, leading to smoother skin.

Co2 lasers are used for:

  • Skin bumps
  • Scars and pores
  • Wrinkles, fine lines

Ultherapy / HIFU

Ultherapy© / HIFU (Lifthera and Ultracel Q) is a non-invasive procedure that lifts the neck, chin and brow, and improves lines and wrinkles on the upper chest.

Alma Hair Removal

Alma hair removal is a revolutionary method that utilizes advanced technology to effectively eliminate unwanted hair from various parts of the body. Using a combination of intense pulsed light (IPL) and radiofrequency (RF) energies, Alma hair removal offers a safe and virtually painless solution for long-term hair reduction.

This innovative technique targets hair follicles, damaging them to inhibit future growth while leaving surrounding skin undamaged. With Alma hair removal, individuals can enjoy smoother, silkier skin without the hassle of frequent shaving or waxing, providing a convenient and reliable solution for achieving lasting hair free results
