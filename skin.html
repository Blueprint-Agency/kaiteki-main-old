Birthmark

Birthmarks are congenital irregularity on the skin present at birth. They can occur anywhere and can be of different colours like brown, red, black or even blue. These irregularities can be formed from abnormal pigments or blood vessels causing emotional unease.

Vascular Lesions

Vascular Lesions is a condition in which tiny blood vessels cause threadlike red lines or patterns on the skin. They are sometimes known as spider veins. They can appear linear or stellate crimson-purple markings on anywhere of the body.

Excessive sweating

Hyperhidrosis or excessive sweating can be uncomfortable and embarrassing. This can be due to overstimulation of the nerves that control the sweat glands and thus excess sweat is produced. It is a common ailment that affects underarms, hands and feet. If you suffer from this condition, you may find yourself sweating even in at rest or at a cool temperature. If this affect your self-esteem, say no more! We are here to help!

The skin is the largest organ in our body.
